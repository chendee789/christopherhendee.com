Experience

Test Engineer

Electric Hydrogen · August 2024 – Present

Lead controls engineer for four 800kW product-scale PEM electrolysis test systems. Work spans troubleshooting test stand trips, fixing production code, leading code reviews, and designing system improvements.

  • Hardware & subsystem design — built a subsystem that dries, cools, and measures produced hydrogen gas to a 0.4% faradaic efficiency accuracy.
  • Safety engineering — evaluated system safety through HAZOP and LOPA analysis and developed safety logic to meet SIL ratings.
  • Tooling — built a modular, CSV-driven test scripting tool that runs each test system independently, significantly cutting the operational burden on test operators.
  • Systems integration — integrated third-party equipment into the control architecture via Ethernet/IP, Modbus, ASCII, and BACnet, including a JCI building management system and retrofitted 250kW rectifiers.
  • Infrastructure rollout — organized the rollout of new test stands into the network and FactoryTalk/Ignition infrastructure, helping drive systems to 97% uptime across a mix of novel R&D and life testing.
  • Field commissioning — traveled repeatedly to Texas to commission a 100MW PEM electrolysis plant, taking ownership of field instrumentation commissioning, cause-and-effect verification, and SIF validation, and writing the documentation handed off to the customer.
